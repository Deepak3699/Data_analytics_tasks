## Objective

Analyze business performance using key performance indicators (KPIs) such as Churn Rate, ROI, and Customer Lifetime Value (LTV).

## KPIs Evaluated

### Churn Rate

Measures the percentage of customers lost during a period.

Formula:

Churn Rate = (Customers Lost / Customers Start) × 100

### ROI

Measures the profitability of marketing investments.

Formula:

ROI = ((Revenue - Marketing Cost) / Marketing Cost) × 100

### Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

Measures the average value generated per customer.

Formula:

LTV = Revenue / Customers Start

## Key Findings

1. Revenue increased steadily from $50,000 in January to $115,000 in December.
2. Customer acquisition outpaced customer losses.
3. ROI remained positive throughout the year.
4. Customer Lifetime Value improved as revenue increased.

## Conclusion

The business demonstrated strong growth in revenue and customer base while maintaining positive returns on marketing investments.
